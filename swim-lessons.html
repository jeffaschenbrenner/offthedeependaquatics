Our programs

Every Swimmer Has a Starting Point

Whether your child has never touched a pool or you're an adult ready to finally learn, we'll meet you exactly where you are.

👶

Parent & Child

Introduce your little one to the water in a safe, playful setting. A parent stays in the water throughout the session to support and bond.

Ages 6 mo – 3 yr
🧒

Kids Beginner

Learn floating, kicking, and basic strokes in a fun and encouraging small-group environment. Water safety is always woven in.

Ages 3 – 7
🏅

Intermediate

Refine stroke technique across all four styles, build confidence in the deep end, and develop stamina and proper breathing.

Ages 7+
🧑

Adult Lessons

It's never too late to learn. Private and small-group adult lessons are available year-round in a judgment-free environment.

All adults
