Qualifications

What We Look For

We welcome instructors at various experience levels. Here's what makes a great candidate:

Aquatic fitness certification

AEA, AFAA, ACE, or equivalent water aerobics or fitness certification is preferred.

CPR / First Aid certified

Current CPR and First Aid certification required or willingness to obtain prior to teaching.

Experience with adult learners

Comfortable leading groups of adults with varying fitness levels, ages, and abilities.

Enthusiastic & reliable

A positive, encouraging energy and strong commitment to showing up for your classes.

Not yet certified?
We encourage interested candidates to reach out even if you're working toward your certification. We'd love to hear from you.
